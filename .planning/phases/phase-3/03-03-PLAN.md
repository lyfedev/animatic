<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Review the generated art against the D-09 failure modes and stage it for sign-off</name>
  <files>output/assets/generated/, .planning/phases/phase-3/03-ART-REVIEW.md</files>
  <action>
Open the generated art and judge it yourself before handing it to the developer. Sample at
least four: the highest-priority location `int_blue_door_fight_club`, one exterior
(`ext_street` or `ext_rockys_apartment`), one bespoke character (`black_fighter` or
`promoter`), and `generic_minor_character`.

Score each image against the five points below, which are the failure modes D-09 exists to
suppress plus the project's own style rule:

1. **Flat black linework on white.** Solid black outlines, even line weight, open white
   space where a surface would otherwise be darkened. The 2026-08-24 smoke test came back
   greyscale with heavy shading — that is the failure this checks for.
2. **The drawing sits alone in the frame.** No panel border, no notebook binding, no page
   texture, no caption block. The smoke test produced spiral binding and a caption block;
   D-09 names the vocabulary that caused it and `STYLE_BLOCK` avoids it.
3. **No words drawn into the picture.** The smoke test rendered an instruction phrase into
   the image as artwork. Any readable text not asked for as in-scene signage is a failure.
4. **Character heads carry no facial features** (PROJECT.md §Visual Style — no facial
   features in wide and medium shots; a full-figure reference sheet is one of those). The
   smoke test did respect this rule, so a regression here means the style block lost it.
5. **The locations read as the script's places**, not generic rooms — the fight club as the
   smoky club of scenes 1-2, the apartment exterior as a street-facing facade.

Where a point fails, fix `STYLE_BLOCK` or the subject clause in `src/animatic/core/style.py`
and re-run `python scripts/build_assets.py --force`, then re-judge. Iterate here rather than
handing on art that every Phase 4 panel will inherit. Ship the best first pass — the bar is
"these read as the intended house style", not perfection, and a second regeneration pass is
the sensible ceiling before the 2026-09-09 deadline.

Write `03-ART-REVIEW.md` in the phase directory recording, per sampled image, the five
verdicts, any regeneration performed and what changed in the style block. That file is the
evidence the developer reviews at the end-of-phase gate, so it must say what was actually
looked at, not that a check was run.
