19 beats — 1 establishing (wide), 8 action (medium) and 10 dialogue (close-up) — at roughly
ten seconds each, so about three and a half minutes. `s2b7` is already cached from 04-01 and
should reuse without a call unless the clause has changed since.

Then open the images and judge them. Score each sampled panel against these six points:

1. **Flat black linework on white.** Solid outlines at one even weight, open white where a
   surface would otherwise be shaded. The project's first smoke test came back greyscale
   with heavy shading; that is the failure this checks for (ROADMAP criterion 2).
2. **The drawing sits alone in the frame.** No panel border, no notebook binding, no page
   texture, no caption block.
3. **No words drawn into the picture.** Any readable text that was not asked for as in-scene
   signage is a failure. Scene 2 is where this risk lives: `s2b1` is the SUPERIMPOSE beat
   whose text was once painted across a frame in 60pt type, and panels render rooms, which
   Phase 3's leak guard never covered (D-12).
4. **Wide and medium heads carry no facial features** — the face is one blank plane
   (PROJECT.md §Visual Style, D-05).
5. **Close-ups carry a brow line, a mouth line and a nose and nothing else** — the eyes stay
   blank. This is the novel clause and the reason this plan exists (ROADMAP criterion 3).
6. **The panel reads as this beat's action in this room.** A character who reads as the wrong
   world is the thin-scene-context failure mode carried forward as D-11 — it hits panels the
   same way it hit slots.

Sample at minimum `s2b1` (wide, the SUPERIMPOSE beat, and the one beat in the scene naming
no characters — so it exercises the branch where no facial clause is emitted at all), `s2b2`
(medium, two characters in the ring), `s2b7` (close-up, one character), `s2b16` (medium, the
knockout) and `s2b18` (close-up, the announcer, who resolves to the shared minor-character
identity).

Where a point fails, fix the clause in `src/animatic/core/panel_prompt.py`, bump
`PROMPT_TEMPLATE_VERSION`, and re-run `--scene 2` — the version is part of the cache key, so
the re-run redraws by itself with no `--force`. Keep the three prompt rules intact while
fixing: state the rule as positive prose, put the rule that matters last, and name no object
that is not wanted in the picture (D-06, D-07). Expect the close-up clause specifically to
need at least one pass; research flagged its wording [ASSUMED] and it has no precedent here.

**Ceiling: two revision passes.** The bar is "these read as the intended house style", not
perfection, and the deadline is 2026-09-09. Anything still wrong after the second pass gets
logged in `.planning/WINDOWS.md` and carried, not chased.

Write `.planning/phases/phase-4/04-ART-REVIEW.md` recording, per sampled panel, the six
verdicts, every clause revision made and what changed between passes. That file is the
evidence the end-of-phase gate reads, so it must say what was actually looked at — not that
a check was run. `output/` is gitignored: commit the clause revision and the review, record
the S3 URIs, and do not commit the images.
